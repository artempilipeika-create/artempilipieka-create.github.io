# Z1 display cabinets

Six exact FR3D donors form one PN-600 catalogue card. Source identities and SHA-256 are in `backend/v2/cabinet_assets/planner/vitrine-variants.json`.

- Lighting left/right/both and opening left/right are independent. A choice selects the corresponding real donor, retaining scene identity, materials, placement and glass shelf count.
- Measured default: 600 × 2000 × 600 mm, base 100, body 1900. Front projection is 22.5 mm. LDSP body and inset back are 18 mm.
- Glass: 568.5 × 1868.5 × 4 mm; original shelf 556 × 580 × 4 mm, center Y1043. Frame outer 596 × 1896, gap 2 mm.
- Groove: width 4, depth 8, Y100..2000, Z309 from rear. LED quantity is based on the full 1900 mm groove, not just the exposed 1864 mm between bottom/top. Cut quantity is rounded per strip to 25 mm. Actual installation segment requires confirmation.
- Z1 black: cuts 1896 × 2 + 596 × 2 = 4.984 m. Stock bars 6 m; no fabricated stock-count rule. Procurement needs an order-wide cutting plan.
- Z1 seal: 4.874 m estimated from glass perimeter. Seal section/glass compatibility, article, allowance and retail minimum are not supplied. Do not round to the supplier's listed 500 m length.
- Four Z1 corner connectors per rectangular frame are separate estimated assembly hardware. Supplier does not confirm included screws.
- AQ LED-LINE 15.0341, 12 V, 7.5 W/m, neutral white: 1.9 / 3.8 m by selected sides. Power supply/control selection remains explicit and unpriced.
- Native KUBIC count is four per shelf. No screws are separately present under shelf/KUBIC in any donor. The native 20 screws article 20646 belong to legs/clips and remain fixed.
- Profile and seal are not treated as sheet materials. Native hinges are not charged twice. Missing prices remain null.
- Glass and profile stay out of the LDSP cutting-order rows. Their dimensions and quantities remain in the manufacturing brief, PDF and native export.
- Added glass shelves are rendered, saved and estimated; native export explicitly requires manual shelf/KUBIC placement in BAZIS, as the native file retains its one original shelf.

## Source constraint

The corrected both-sides/left-opening file `(1)` has a 600 × 2000 × 600 nominal frame but no model-level elasticity. That one variant is fixed to its default dimensions. The other five have X455, Y1770 and equal Z160/Z445 elastic planes. Original shelf Y1043 remains below the Y plane. Native BAZIS runtime is unavailable here: no claim of a live BAZIS import test.

## Verification

Independent native-panel fixtures cover all six donors. Tests cover all 36 choice transitions, resizing, fixed-size guarding, transparent glass/black frame/light geometry, shelving, pricing, actual native-export output, LDSP-only order transfer, Python/JS quantity parity, schema JSON round trips and PDF content. No real orders, messages, production database or private production files are touched.

PDF output was rendered and visually inspected. Browser QA was not run: the Sites skill's managed-environment browser capability was unavailable. This release changes only the existing isolated staging service and adds no schema migration.
